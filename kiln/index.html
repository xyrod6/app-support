Support and frequently asked questions.

Need help? Get in touch through signex.ai/contact and mention Kiln. We read everything.

How many habits and reminders can I have?

Unlimited habits and unlimited reminders per habit, free. Habits can be yes/no, a count, or a duration, on daily, specific-weekday or N-times-per-week schedules.

Does skipping an unscheduled day break my streak?

No. Streaks are schedule-aware — a habit set for Monday, Wednesday and Friday only cares about those days, and the days in between neither help nor hurt.

Why don't the two completion percentages in Reports match?

They measure differently. The headline figure averages each habit's own completion rate, so every habit counts equally. The weekday panel pools all scheduled days together, so habits with more scheduled days weigh more. Both are correct; they answer different questions, and they will not always agree.

A reminder didn't fire. Why?

First check that notifications are allowed under Settings › Notifications › Kiln. Beyond that, iOS limits every app to 64 pending notifications, so with many habits and many reminders Kiln schedules the nearest ones first and tops the schedule up each time you open the app. Opening Kiln now and then keeps far-future reminders flowing.

How do I keep my habit history private?

Turn on the Face ID lock in Settings. Kiln will then require Face ID or your passcode each time it opens. Nothing is sent anywhere either way.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. CSV export is built in and free, and habits can be archived rather than deleted if you want to keep their history.
