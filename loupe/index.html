Support and frequently asked questions.

Need help? Get in touch through signex.ai/contact and mention Loupe. We read everything.

The image shakes too much to read

Freeze it. Tap the freeze button once, then pan and zoom the still at leisure — that is the feature built for reading a menu or a label without holding your hand steady. The last six frozen frames are kept during the session so you can compare two labels.

How do I turn on the light?

The torch button turns it on at half power; the level slider is in Adjust if you want more or less. On devices without a torch the control is hidden. The torch switches off when you leave the app, so it never burns in your pocket.

What are the colour filters for?

Seven presets for low vision: natural, high contrast, greyscale, inverted, yellow-on-black, black-on-yellow and blue-on-white. Filters apply identically to the live view and to frozen frames, alongside brightness and extra contrast.

Why are my frozen frames gone when I reopen the app?

Frozen frames live in memory for the session only. To keep one, share it — the share button saves it wherever you choose, including your photo library.

Does it work with VoiceOver and large text?

Yes — that is the point of the app. Every control is labelled with hints, targets are large, text scales to the biggest accessibility sizes without truncation, and Reduce Motion, Increase Contrast and Bold Text are respected. The zoom slider announces its level, as in "3.2 times".

Why does it want camera access?

The camera is the magnifying glass — there is nothing to show without it. If you declined the prompt, the app shows a working shortcut to Settings where you can allow it.
