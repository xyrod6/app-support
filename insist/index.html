How is this different from an ordinary reminder?

Insist keeps asking. A reminder re-alerts every 1 to 60 minutes — your choice — until you mark it done or snooze it, straight from the notification. One alert you can swipe away is exactly the problem this app exists to solve.

Why did the nagging eventually stop?

iOS keeps at most 64 pending notifications per app and never wakes a closed app to schedule more, so each reminder is scheduled as a chain of alerts up front, with the nearest-due reminders given the longest chains. Opening the app tops every chain back up. If your reminders genuinely cannot fit in the budget, Insist tells you so on screen rather than failing silently.

It misread the time I typed

The parse is shown back to you live before you save — "tomorrow 9am", "in 20 minutes", "on the 1st" — and anything it got wrong is one tap to correct. If a phrasing reliably fails, send it to us; the parser grows by real examples.

Do Done and Snooze work if the app is closed?

Yes. Both actions work from the notification itself, even cold — marking done cancels the rest of that reminder's chain immediately.

Notifications aren't appearing at all

Check Settings › Notifications › Insist is allowed, and that a Focus mode is not silencing it. Insist shows a clear screen inside the app when permission is missing.

What happens to a monthly reminder set for the 31st?

It clamps to the end of shorter months — the 31st, then 28 February, then back to the 31st of March.
