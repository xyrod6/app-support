Privacy Policy

Repeat Reminders: Insist · last updated 2 September 2026

Insist does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. The app makes no network requests.

Notifications

Insist asks for notification permission because notifications are the product — each reminder is a chain of alerts scheduled locally on your device through the system's notification centre. The text you type into a reminder appears in those notifications and nowhere else. No notification service of ours is involved because none exists.

Natural-language entry

When you type "tomorrow at 9", the phrase is parsed on your device by Apple's built-in text analysis and the app's own rules. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

What is stored on your device

Your reminders, lists and preferences are saved in the app's own private storage on your iPhone. This never leaves the device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

The app shows no third-party advertising, contains no ad network, requests no identifier for advertisers (IDFA) and runs no analytics. If advertising is introduced in a future version, this policy and the app's App Store privacy details will be updated first.

Children

Because the app collects nothing at all, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
