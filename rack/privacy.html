Privacy Policy

Gym Workout Log: Rack · last updated 2 September 2026

Rack does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. The app makes no network requests.

Health data

If you allow it, Rack saves your completed workouts to the Health app so they appear in Apple Fitness. It reads nothing from Health. This is optional and logging works fully without it. Workout data is written on your device, is never transmitted anywhere, is never used for advertising, and is never shared with anyone. You can withdraw access at any time in Settings › Health.

Notifications

Rack asks for notification permission so the rest timer can alert you when it ends while the screen is off. Notifications are scheduled locally on your device; no notification service of ours is involved because none exists.

What is stored on your device

Your routines, exercises, sessions, personal records and preferences are saved in the app's own private storage on your iPhone. CSV files you export go only where you choose to share them. Nothing leaves the device otherwise, and everything is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

The app shows no third-party advertising, contains no ad network, requests no identifier for advertisers (IDFA) and runs no analytics. If advertising is introduced in a future version, this policy and the app's App Store privacy details will be updated first.

Children

Because the app collects nothing at all, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
