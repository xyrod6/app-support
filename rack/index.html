Gym Workout Log: Rack

Support and frequently asked questions.

Need help? Get in touch through signex.ai/contact and mention Rack. We read everything.

How many routines can I have?

As many as you like. Routines, exercises and history are all unlimited and free — there is no cap and nothing to unlock.

My exercise isn't in the library

The bundled library has 155 exercises with search and filters. If yours is missing, add it as a custom lift — it then behaves exactly like a built-in one, with its own history and progression charts.

How does the rest timer notify me if my screen is off?

The first time you use the rest timer, Rack asks for notification permission so it can post a local notification when the timer ends while the screen is off or the app is in the background. If you decline, the timer still runs and buzzes while the app is open.

Where do the "previous session" numbers come from?

From your last session that included that exercise, shown inline so you know what to beat. One thing to be aware of: a session you started and abandoned without finishing still counts as your last session, so occasionally the reference numbers come from a workout you never completed.

What is the plate calculator doing?

It breaks a target bar weight into the plates to load per side. It sits inline in the active session so you never leave the set you are on.

Do my workouts show up in Apple Fitness?

They can. If you allow Health access, Rack saves each completed workout to the Health app, and Apple Fitness picks it up from there. This is optional — logging works fully without it.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Full CSV export is built in and free.
