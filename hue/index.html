Support and frequently asked questions.

Need help? Get in touch through signex.ai/contact and mention Hue. We read everything.

How do I log a mood?

Two taps: pick one of the five moods — the entry is saved on that first tap — then tap what you were doing. A note and a photo are optional extras, never required.

Where does my backup go?

Hue automatically writes your whole journal as a plain JSON file to your own iCloud Drive, in the app's Documents folder. There is no account and no server of ours — it is your iCloud. If iCloud is unavailable, the backup is kept on the iPhone instead. You can also export the same file through the share sheet and import it on another device.

How do I lock the journal?

Turn on the lock in Settings and Hue will require Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode each time it opens. This keeps entries private on a shared or unlocked phone.

Why does it say there isn't enough data for correlations?

Correlations are suppressed until a mood and an activity have each been logged on at least five days. Below that, any pattern would be noise. What Hue shows is plain co-occurrence stated in sentences — it is not a diagnosis and makes no health claim.

Can I rename moods or add my own activities?

Yes, both. Mood names are editable and custom activities are unlimited, as are weekly goals.

Does Hue see my photo library?

No. Attaching a photo uses the system photo picker, which runs outside the app — Hue only ever receives the single photo you choose, and never browses your library.

Is my journal synced live between devices?

No. The iCloud file is a backup, not live sync. To move to a new phone, import the backup file there.
