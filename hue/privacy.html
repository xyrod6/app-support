Privacy Policy

Mood Journal: Hue · last updated 5 September 2026

Hue does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. Your journal is stored on your device and in your own iCloud — never on any server of ours, because we have none.

Your journal

Entries, notes, photos, activities and goals are saved in the app's own private storage on your iPhone. All analysis — charts, streaks, correlations — happens on the device. Nothing is transmitted to us or to any third party.

iCloud backup

Hue automatically writes a backup of your journal as a JSON file to the app's own folder in your iCloud Drive, under your Apple account and covered by Apple's iCloud terms. We cannot read it. If iCloud is unavailable the backup is kept on the device instead. Files you export yourself through the share sheet go only where you send them.

Photos

Attaching a photo to an entry uses the system photo picker, which runs outside the app. Hue receives only the single photo you choose and never has access to your photo library as a whole, so no photo library permission is requested.

Face ID

If you turn on the lock, Hue asks to use Face ID so your entries stay private on a shared or unlocked phone. Authentication is handled entirely by iOS — the app never sees your face data, which never leaves the device's secure hardware.

Advertising

The app shows a small promotional strip for our own products on a few screens. It is drawn by the app itself from a fixed, built-in list — there is no ad network, no ad SDK, no identifier for advertisers (IDFA), no analytics and no third-party advertising of any kind. Nothing about you is collected, read or sent in order to choose or display it, and no information about you is shared with anyone. If you tap it, iOS opens the linked page in Safari or the App Store; the link carries only which of our apps you tapped it in, so we can tell whether these are worth showing at all. If third-party advertising is ever introduced, this policy and the app's App Store privacy details will be updated first.

Children

Because the app collects nothing at all, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
