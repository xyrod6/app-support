Step Counter: Stride

Support and frequently asked questions.

Need help? Get in touch through signex.ai/contact and mention Stride. We read everything.

Where do my steps come from?

From your phone. Stride reads the step count, distance and flights climbed that your iPhone's motion coprocessor already records, and it reads your longer history from the Health app so charts and streaks can go back further than the phone's own motion log. It does not count steps itself, and it does not run in the background.

Why does my count differ from my Watch or another app?

Different sources record slightly different numbers. If your history comes from Health, Stride shows what Health has — which may combine phone and Watch data using Health's own rules. Small disagreements between apps reading the same day are normal.

I denied motion or Health access. Can I change my mind?

Yes. Motion access is under Settings › Privacy & Security › Motion & Fitness, and Health access is under Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices. Stride shows a clear screen when access is missing, with a shortcut to the right place.

How do streaks and personal bests work?

A streak counts consecutive days on which you met your step goal, which you can edit at any time. Personal bests track your highest single days. Both are computed from your recorded history, so a longer Health history means fairer bests.

Can I get my data out?

Yes — CSV export is built in and free. It produces a file you can share anywhere.

Is anything locked behind a purchase?

No. All four chart windows, the year heatmap, themes and export are unlocked.

Does it need an account or an internet connection?

Neither. Everything runs on your iPhone, offline.
