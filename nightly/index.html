Support and frequently asked questions.

Need help? Get in touch through signex.ai/contact and mention Nightly. We read everything.

Nightly shows no sleep. Why?

Nightly does not record sleep itself — it reads the sleep your iPhone or Apple Watch already logs to the Health app. If Health has no sleep data, Nightly has nothing to show. Check that sleep tracking is on (a Watch worn at night, or Sleep set up on the iPhone), and that Nightly has read access under Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices.

How is sleep debt calculated?

As a rolling 14-night balance against your sleep need, which you can set yourself. Nights where you slept less than your need add to the debt; longer nights pay it down. It is an arithmetic summary of your recorded sleep, not a medical assessment.

What is the energy curve?

An estimate of how alert you are likely to feel through today, computed from your real bed and wake times using a standard two-process model of sleep pressure and body clock. It is an estimate — caffeine, naps and life are not in the model.

Why didn't the smart alarm wake me early?

The smart alarm always schedules a backstop notification for the exact time you set, so it cannot be missed. Ringing earlier, in a light sleep stage, needs live stage data — in practice an Apple Watch — and the app needs to have been opened recently enough to check. Without a Watch, the alarm is simply the time you set.

Can I edit a night that was recorded wrongly?

Not in Nightly — it reads Health and deliberately writes nothing back. Edit the night in the Health app and Nightly will pick up the correction.

My Watch and phone both recorded last night. Is it counted twice?

No. Overlapping records from multiple sources are deduplicated before anything is calculated, so a night covered by both devices counts once.

Does it need an account or an internet connection?

Neither. Everything is computed on your iPhone, offline.
