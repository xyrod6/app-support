Privacy Policy

Sleep Debt Tracker: Nightly · last updated 5 September 2026

Nightly does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. The app makes no network requests.

Health data

Nightly asks to read the sleep your iPhone or Apple Watch already records in the Health app, so it can show last night, your sleep debt and your energy curve. It is read-only: nothing is written back to Health. Sleep data is read and processed entirely on your device, is never transmitted anywhere, is never used for advertising, and is never shared with anyone. You can withdraw access at any time in Settings › Health.

Notifications

If you set an alarm, Nightly asks for notification permission so the alarm can ring. All notifications are scheduled locally on your device.

What is stored on your device

Your sleep need, alarm settings and preferences are saved in the app's own private storage on your iPhone. This never leaves the device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

The app shows a small promotional strip for our own products on a few screens. It is drawn by the app itself from a fixed, built-in list — there is no ad network, no ad SDK, no identifier for advertisers (IDFA), no analytics and no third-party advertising of any kind. Nothing about you is collected, read or sent in order to choose or display it, and no information about you is shared with anyone. If you tap it, iOS opens the linked page in Safari or the App Store; the link carries only which of our apps you tapped it in, so we can tell whether these are worth showing at all. If third-party advertising is ever introduced, this policy and the app's App Store privacy details will be updated first.

Children

Because the app collects nothing at all, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
