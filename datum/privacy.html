Privacy Policy

Datum: Field Plans & Takeoffs · last updated 16 September 2026

Datum does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. The app makes no network requests.

Camera

Datum asks for camera access to measure distances and scan rooms with AR. Camera frames are processed on your device by Apple's ARKit and RoomPlan frameworks and then discarded — nothing is recorded, nothing is saved as video or photos, and nothing is uploaded. What is kept is only the geometry: the points, lengths and room shapes you capture.

What is stored on your device

Your projects, rooms, measurements and preferences are saved in the app's own private storage on your iPhone. PDF, CSV, DXF, and USDZ files you export go only where you choose to share them. Nothing leaves the device otherwise, and everything is deleted when you delete the app.

Network access

Datum has no account system, analytics, advertising, or cloud service. It does not make network requests. Your projects, rooms, measurements, and preferences remain in the app's private on-device storage; exported files go only where you choose to share them.

Children

Because the app collects nothing at all, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
