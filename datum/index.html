Support and frequently asked questions.

Need help? Get in touch through signex.ai/contact and mention Datum. We read everything.

Room scanning says it's unavailable on my iPhone

Room scanning uses Apple's RoomPlan, which needs a LiDAR sensor — the Pro iPhones and Pro iPads have one, the standard models do not. On a non-LiDAR device the measuring tape still works fully; only the room scan is off the table, and the app says so rather than pretending.

How accurate are the measurements?

As accurate as ARKit's tracking, which in good conditions is within a centimetre or two over room distances. Texture and light help; blank walls, glass, mirrors and dim rooms hurt. Treat it as a planning tape, and check anything you are about to cut with a real one.

What can I export?

A drafting-sheet PDF, a CSV quantity schedule, a DXF drawing, and a USDZ 3D model you can open on any Apple device or send to CAD tools that read USD.

Is there a limit on measurements or rooms?

No. Measuring, projects, rooms, and exports are available without an account or cloud service.

Tips for a better scan?

Open the curtains, move slowly, keep the camera pointed at walls rather than the middle of the room, and walk the full perimeter including corners. Close mirrored doors if you can — mirrors read as extra rooms to any scanner.

Can I switch between metric and imperial?

Yes, at any time — existing measurements redisplay in the new unit, and you can edit and rename measurements after capture.
