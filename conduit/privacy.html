Privacy Policy

Network File Browser: Conduit · last updated 2 September 2026

Conduit talks only to the servers you add. There are no accounts with us, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. The app has no server of its own and never contacts one — every connection it makes is to an address you typed in yourself.

Your servers and credentials

When you add a connection, its address and username are saved in the app's private storage and its password is saved in the iOS Keychain, the system's encrypted credential store. Credentials never leave your device except to authenticate with the server you configured — they are never sent to us or to any third party, because there is nowhere to send them. Deleting a connection deletes its password from the Keychain.

Network connections

Conduit connects over WebDAV or FTP/FTPS to the servers you add, to list, stream and transfer files at your request. iOS asks for local-network permission so servers on your own network — a NAS, router or home server — are reachable. Nothing about your connections, files or activity is reported anywhere. Note that plain FTP is unencrypted by design of the protocol; prefer FTPS or WebDAV over HTTPS where your server offers it.

What is stored on your device

Saved connections, downloaded files, the transfer queue and preferences are kept in the app's own private storage on your iPhone, and are deleted when you delete the app. Passwords live in the Keychain as described above.

Advertising

The app shows no third-party advertising, contains no ad network, requests no identifier for advertisers (IDFA) and runs no analytics. If advertising is introduced in a future version, this policy and the app's App Store privacy details will be updated first.

Children

Conduit collects nothing about anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
