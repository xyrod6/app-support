Privacy Policy

Solunar Fishing: Moonrun · last updated 2 September 2026

Moonrun does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. The app makes no network requests — all astronomy is computed on your device.

Location

Moonrun asks for your location, while you are using the app, to compute the moon and sun times for where you are fishing or hunting. Your location is used on the device for that calculation and nothing else. It is never transmitted anywhere and never stored beyond the spots and catches you choose to save yourself.

What is stored on your device

Your saved spots, catch log and preferences are saved in the app's own private storage on your iPhone. This never leaves the device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

The app shows no third-party advertising, contains no ad network, requests no identifier for advertisers (IDFA) and runs no analytics. If advertising is introduced in a future version, this policy and the app's App Store privacy details will be updated first.

Children

Because the app collects nothing at all, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
