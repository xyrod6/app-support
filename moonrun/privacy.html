Privacy Policy

Solunar Fishing: Moonrun · last updated 5 September 2026

Moonrun does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. All astronomy is computed on your device. The app makes one kind of network request: when you save a location, it asks Apple's geocoder for that place's name. Nothing else in the app uses the network, and it works fully with the network switched off.

Location

Moonrun asks for your location, while you are using the app, to compute the moon and sun times for where you are fishing or hunting. Your location is used on the device for that calculation and nothing else. It is never transmitted anywhere and never stored beyond the spots and catches you choose to save yourself.

What is stored on your device

Your saved spots, catch log and preferences are saved in the app's own private storage on your iPhone. This never leaves the device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

The app shows a small promotional strip for our own products on a few screens. It is drawn by the app itself from a fixed, built-in list — there is no ad network, no ad SDK, no identifier for advertisers (IDFA), no analytics and no third-party advertising of any kind. Nothing about you is collected, read or sent in order to choose or display it, and no information about you is shared with anyone. If you tap it, iOS opens the linked page in Safari or the App Store; the link carries only which of our apps you tapped it in, so we can tell whether these are worth showing at all. If third-party advertising is ever introduced, this policy and the app's App Store privacy details will be updated first.

Children

Because the app collects nothing at all, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
