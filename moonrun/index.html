Support and frequently asked questions.

What are majors and minors?

The classic solunar feeding periods. Majors are two-hour windows centred on the moon's upper and lower transit — when it crosses overhead or underfoot. Minors are one-hour windows centred on moonrise and moonset. Moonrun computes all four from lunar position, offline.

How is the day rating worked out?

From three documented parts, combined into a star rating, and shown honestly as a heuristic. The full method is in the app under the rating — tap it to read exactly what goes in. Fish have not read the method, so treat it as a tilt of the odds, not a promise.

Why do my saved spot's times look wrong?

Times for a saved spot are shown in your device's current time zone, not the spot's own. Checking a distant spot from home, the events are correct but the clock is yours — worth remembering when planning a trip across time zones.

Are there tide tables?

No, by design. Reliable tide data is licensed and regional, and a wrong tide is worse than none. Moonrun sticks to what can be computed exactly anywhere: sun, moon, and the solunar periods.

What does the catch log record?

Whatever you enter, plus a stamp of the conditions at the moment you save — the active period, moon phase and sun and moon times — so you can look back and see which conditions produced fish for you. It stays on your phone.

Does it work with no signal at the water?

Yes, completely. Everything is computed on the device from a built-in astronomical engine; the app makes no network requests at all.

Do I have to share my location?

Only if you want times for where you are standing. You can decline and use saved spots by coordinates instead.
