Support and frequently asked questions.

Why does the trend line ignore my latest weigh-in?

It doesn't ignore it — it weights it. The heavy line is an exponentially weighted moving average of your weigh-ins, because the day-to-day number is mostly water. Your raw weigh-ins stay visible as dots; the line is the part worth watching.

Why won't it give me a goal date?

The projection refuses to guess when your data is too noisy, flat, or moving away from the goal. A date it cannot stand behind would be worse than no date, so in those cases it says so instead.

How do I pull in weights from my smart scale?

If your scale writes to the Health app, allow Baseline's Health access and tap Import in Settings. Import is manual — Baseline does not watch Health in the background, so tap Import again after new weigh-ins arrive.

Do weights I log here reach my other apps?

If you allow Health write access, yes — weights you log in Baseline are saved to Health, where your other apps can read them. Both directions are optional.

Which units are supported?

Kilograms, pounds, and stones and pounds, throughout the app.

What does the weekly rate mean?

The slope of your trend line over the recent past, with a confidence guard: when the trend is too uncertain, the rate is withheld rather than shown as false precision. Plateau detection works the same way — it reports one only when the data supports it.
