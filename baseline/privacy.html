Privacy Policy

Weight Trend: Baseline · last updated 2 September 2026

Baseline does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. The app makes no network requests.

Health data

If you choose to connect the Health app, Baseline asks to read body mass already recorded by your scale or another app, and to write the weights you log in Baseline back to Health. Both are optional. Health data is read and written on your device, is never transmitted anywhere, is never used for advertising, and is never shared with anyone. You can withdraw access at any time in Settings › Health.

What is stored on your device

Your weight entries, milestones, goal and preferences are saved in the app's own private storage on your iPhone. This never leaves the device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

The app shows no third-party advertising, contains no ad network, requests no identifier for advertisers (IDFA) and runs no analytics. If advertising is introduced in a future version, this policy and the app's App Store privacy details will be updated first.

Children

Because the app collects nothing at all, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
