Breathing Exercises: Bellows

Support and frequently asked questions.

Need help? Get in touch through signex.ai/contact and mention Bellows. We read everything.

Which breathing patterns are included?

Ten, including box breathing, 4-7-8, coherent breathing and the double-inhale sigh, plus an unlimited custom builder with a live preview. A pattern is just its timings — every description says what the pattern is, and none of them makes a health claim.

What is screen-dark mode?

A session mode for dark rooms where the display goes almost entirely dark and you follow the rhythm by feel — haptics mark the phases and the optional tone rises and falls with your breath. The faint on-screen text is deliberately dim; the haptics and audio carry the cues.

Why did the sound stop when I locked my phone?

A session needs the app in the foreground — there is no background audio in this version. Keep the screen on during a session; screen-dark mode exists precisely so a lit phone does not light the room.

I don't feel the haptic cues

Check that System Haptics are on under Settings › Sounds & Haptics, and that Silent Mode haptics are allowed. On devices without the newer haptic engine, Bellows falls back to simpler taps, which are less textured but still mark every phase.

Do my sessions count as mindful minutes?

If you allow Health access, yes — each session's minutes are written to the Health app as mindful minutes. Bellows reads nothing from Health, and this is optional.

Can it remind me to practise?

Yes, there is an optional daily reminder. It asks for notification permission the first time you turn it on, and the notification is scheduled locally on your phone.
