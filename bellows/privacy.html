Privacy Policy

Breathing Exercises: Bellows · last updated 5 September 2026

Bellows does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. Bellows makes no background network requests.

Health data

If you allow it, Bellows writes the minutes you spend in a breathing session to the Health app as mindful minutes. It reads nothing from Health. This is optional. The data is written on your device, is never transmitted anywhere, is never used for advertising, and is never shared with anyone. You can withdraw access at any time in Settings › Health.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, Bellows asks for notification permission. The reminder is scheduled locally on your device.

Audio

The breathing tones are synthesised inside the app and played out only. The microphone is never used and no microphone permission is requested.

What is stored on your device

Your custom patterns, session history, streaks and preferences are saved in the app's own private storage on your iPhone. This never leaves the device and is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

The app shows a small, clearly labelled promotional strip for our own products on a few screens. It is drawn by Bellows from a fixed list — there is no ad network, ad SDK, IDFA, analytics or third-party advertising. The choice is based only on the device language, with Unfoldbox shown for Hungarian-language users. Nothing about you is collected, read or sent to choose or display it, and HealthKit data is never used for advertising. If you tap a partner link, iOS opens the named page in Safari or the App Store. The destination may have its own privacy policy and server logs. The shelf includes a Report an ad link to Bellows support.

Children

Because the app collects nothing at all, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
