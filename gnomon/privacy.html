Privacy Policy

Golden Hour Sun: Gnomon · last updated 5 September 2026

Gnomon does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. All astronomy is computed on your device. The app makes one kind of network request: when you save a location, it asks Apple's geocoder for that place's name. Nothing else in the app uses the network, and it works fully with the network switched off.

Location

Gnomon asks for your location, while you are using the app, to place the sun and moon correctly for where you are standing, and it uses the compass heading to orient the AR view. Your location is used on the device for that calculation and nothing else. It is never transmitted anywhere and never stored beyond the spots you choose to save yourself.

Camera

The AR view asks for camera access so the sun's path can be drawn over the live image. Camera frames are shown to you and discarded — never recorded, never saved, never transmitted. The plan dome works without the camera entirely.

What is stored on your device

Your saved spots, dates and preferences are saved in the app's own private storage on your iPhone. Share cards you create go only where you choose to send them. Nothing leaves the device otherwise, and everything is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

The app shows a small promotional strip for our own products on a few screens. It is drawn by the app itself from a fixed, built-in list — there is no ad network, no ad SDK, no identifier for advertisers (IDFA), no analytics and no third-party advertising of any kind. Nothing about you is collected, read or sent in order to choose or display it, and no information about you is shared with anyone. If you tap it, iOS opens the linked page in Safari or the App Store; the link carries only which of our apps you tapped it in, so we can tell whether these are worth showing at all. If third-party advertising is ever introduced, this policy and the app's App Store privacy details will be updated first.

Children

Because the app collects nothing at all, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
