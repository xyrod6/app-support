Privacy Policy

Golden Hour Sun: Gnomon · last updated 2 September 2026

Gnomon does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. The app makes no network requests — all astronomy is computed on your device.

Location

Gnomon asks for your location, while you are using the app, to place the sun and moon correctly for where you are standing, and it uses the compass heading to orient the AR view. Your location is used on the device for that calculation and nothing else. It is never transmitted anywhere and never stored beyond the spots you choose to save yourself.

Camera

The AR view asks for camera access so the sun's path can be drawn over the live image. Camera frames are shown to you and discarded — never recorded, never saved, never transmitted. The plan dome works without the camera entirely.

What is stored on your device

Your saved spots, dates and preferences are saved in the app's own private storage on your iPhone. Share cards you create go only where you choose to send them. Nothing leaves the device otherwise, and everything is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

The app shows no third-party advertising, contains no ad network, requests no identifier for advertisers (IDFA) and runs no analytics. If advertising is introduced in a future version, this policy and the app's App Store privacy details will be updated first.

Children

Because the app collects nothing at all, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
