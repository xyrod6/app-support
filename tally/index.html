Support and frequently asked questions.

Need help? Get in touch through signex.ai/contact and mention Tally. We read everything.

Does it connect to my bank?

No, and it never will by design. Tally is manual entry: the app makes no network call of any kind, so your finances stay on your phone. In exchange, entry is as fast as we could make it — cold launch lands on the keypad, and a two-digit expense is three taps.

I posted an expense by mistake

You have a four-second undo the moment it posts. After that, find the entry in History and edit or delete it there.

How do foreign currencies work?

You enter the exchange rates yourself — there is no rate feed, because there is no network. One consequence to know: rates are current, not historical, so editing a rate re-values past entries in that currency. If you care about the rate an old trip was booked at, leave that rate alone.

How do budgets handle months of different lengths?

Budget periods and monthly recurring rules keep their anchor day and clamp to short months: an entry anchored on the 31st falls on 28 February and returns to the 31st in March.

Can I stop other people seeing my spending?

Yes. Turn on the Face ID lock in Settings and Tally requires Face ID or your passcode each time it opens.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. History exports to CSV, with the same date, category, account and direction filters applied.

Is anything locked behind a purchase?

No. Categories, accounts, budgets, recurring rules, themes and export are all free and unlimited.
