Privacy Policy

Expense Tracker: Tally · last updated 5 September 2026

Tally does not collect any data. There are no accounts, no bank connections, no analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no third-party trackers. The app makes no network requests — your finances never leave your phone.

Your financial data

Everything you enter — expenses, income, categories, accounts, budgets, recurring rules and exchange rates — is saved in the app's own private storage on your iPhone. There is no bank sync, no rate feed and no server. CSV files you export go only where you choose to share them. Everything is deleted when you delete the app.

Face ID

If you turn on the lock, Tally asks to use Face ID so nobody who picks up your phone can read what you spend. Authentication is handled entirely by iOS — the app never sees your face data, which never leaves the device's secure hardware.

Advertising

The app shows a small promotional strip for our own products on a few screens. It is drawn by the app itself from a fixed, built-in list — there is no ad network, no ad SDK, no identifier for advertisers (IDFA), no analytics and no third-party advertising of any kind. Nothing about you is collected, read or sent in order to choose or display it, and no information about you is shared with anyone. If you tap it, iOS opens the linked page in Safari or the App Store; the link carries only which of our apps you tapped it in, so we can tell whether these are worth showing at all. If third-party advertising is ever introduced, this policy and the app's App Store privacy details will be updated first.

Children

Because the app collects nothing at all, it collects nothing from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
