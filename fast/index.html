Fasting Timer: Fast Zone

Support and frequently asked questions.

Need help? Get in touch through signex.ai/contact and mention Fast Zone. We read everything.

I forgot to start the timer. Can I fix it?

Yes. Start and end times can be edited retroactively everywhere — on the running timer and on any past fast in your history. Tap the time to change it, and your streaks and statistics recalculate.

Which fasting schedules are included?

16:8, 18:6, 20:4, OMAD and 5:2, plus custom schedules with any fasting window you like. There is no limit on how many custom schedules you create.

What do the colours on the timer mean?

The ring passes through six metabolic zones as your fast progresses, with a gentle haptic at each crossing. The zone descriptions say what the body typically does at that stage — they are informational, not medical advice, and everyone's body is different.

Does it remind me when my fast ends?

Not in this version. The timer keeps running whether or not the app is open, so opening it at any point shows exactly where you are, but there are no notifications yet.

How does the weight logging work with the Health app?

You can log weights by hand inside the app. If you allow Health access, Fast Zone can also read your body mass from Health and save the weights you log there, so all your apps see the same numbers. This is optional — decline it and everything else works exactly the same.

Is my history capped unless I pay?

No. History and analytics are unlimited, including the 7, 30 and 90-day duration charts.

Does it need an account or an internet connection?

Neither. Everything runs on your iPhone, offline. There is also no iCloud sync yet, so your history lives on the one device.
