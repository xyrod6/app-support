Support and frequently asked questions.

Need help? Get in touch through signex.ai/contact and mention Plumb. We read everything.

How accurate is the maths?

Exact to 1/64". Plumb does all dimension arithmetic with exact fractions rather than decimals, so 8' 4¾" + 3' 11⅝" comes out exactly right and repeated calculations do not drift the way rounded decimals can.

How do I type feet, inches and fractions?

The keypad has dedicated FT, IN and fraction keys. Enter the whole number, tap the unit, then tap the fraction. The display always shows the result reduced to its simplest form.

Which solvers and takeoffs are included?

Rafter, stair and right-triangle solvers, each with a live diagram that updates as you type, and nine material takeoffs with a waste percentage. One limit to know: the stair solver computes headroom for a straight flight only.

What is the paper tape?

A running record of every calculation, like the tape on a desk calculator. You can share it as text or CSV, and save calculations into named jobs to come back to later.

Can I use it on site with no signal?

Yes. Plumb makes no network requests at all — everything works with no signal, no account and no permissions. The dark theme is designed to stay readable in the sun.

Are my saved jobs synced between devices?

No. Jobs are stored on the device only; there is no cloud sync in this version.
